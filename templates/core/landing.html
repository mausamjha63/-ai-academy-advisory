RESPONSIBLE AI

What We Don't Do

✕

We do not replace official university decisions.

✕

We do not invent academic rules.

✕

We do not guarantee eligibility when required information is missing.

✕

We do not silently resolve conflicting institutional information.

✕

We do not treat incomplete information as certainty.

✕

We do not require real student records for this demonstration.

"When information is insufficient or conflicting, the system acknowledges that limitation rather than presenting unsupported certainty."
